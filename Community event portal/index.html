Community Bulletin

Stay updated with local happenings.

The city council has announced the launch of a brand-new community sports complex near Adyar, set to open by August 2025. The facility will include a swimming pool, indoor badminton courts, and a jogging track open to all residents free of charge.

This year's Annual Music Fest promises over 20 local bands, food stalls, and a special children's zone. Volunteers are welcome — sign up at the community centre or through this portal before July 1st.

The Baking Workshop scheduled for July 14th is almost full. Expert pastry chefs from across Tamil Nadu will lead hands-on sessions for beginners and enthusiasts alike. Register now to secure your spot.

Local NGO GreenCity has partnered with the council to introduce weekly Sunday markets selling organic produce and handmade crafts at Marina Beach Road every Sunday from 7AM to 12PM.

Residents are reminded that the annual pothole repair drive in Mylapore and T. Nagar begins on June 20th. Please report road damage using the CityConnect app or the contact form on this portal.

The Tech Talk series kicks off on July 1st with a session on Cybersecurity for Citizens. Free entry for students with a valid ID. Seats are limited — register through this portal today to avoid disappointment.
